Evaluation Criteria (score each independently, 0-4 integer scale: 0 = fails badly,
1 = poor, 2 = mediocre, 3 = good, 4 = excellent):

1. Manga/Script Follow (PRIMARY — weight this most heavily) — Does the candidate
   faithfully reproduce the action, pose, composition, and camera framing described
   by the Script Clip above AND depicted in the original source manga panel image?
   A candidate that looks beautiful but shows the wrong action/pose/framing must
   score LOW here.
2. Character Identity Consistency — Does the candidate match the character
   reference image(s) (hairstyle, eye color, outfit colors)? If no reference images
   were provided for this panel, judge this against the character description
   implied by the clip text instead, and do not penalize the candidate just for
   lacking a reference image to match.
3. Art Style & Colorization Quality — Is the candidate fully colorized, in a clean
   anime cel-shading style, with NO black-and-white line art or screentone residue,
   and NO speech bubbles or dialogue boxes? These are known failure modes — check
   for them explicitly and score harshly if present.
4. Character Count Accuracy — Does the candidate show the same FOREGROUND,
   individually-distinguishable characters as those listed in "Characters
   appearing in this panel" above — no more, no fewer? This is about specific
   identifiable people: a figure large and detailed enough in the frame that it
   reads as a particular person, not a small, distant, anonymous figure in a
   wide or extreme-wide shot. A crowd in the stands, a scattering of background
   players on a field, silhouettes of pedestrians, or any other background
   population that the Environment/Description text itself describes as part
   of the scene is expected scene-setting content — do NOT count it here, and
   do NOT penalize it, even when "Characters appearing in this panel" is
   "none". Score 0 here ONLY for: an extra foreground person who is not in the
   listed character set and is not accounted for by the Environment/Description
   text, a stray limb (arm/hand) in the foreground that does not belong to any
   listed character's body, or a listed character rendered twice as two
   separate bodies in the same frame.

Important Rules:

* Output JSON only. Do not include markdown.
* For every criterion, write the "reason" field first and let it determine the
  "score" — do not decide the score before justifying it.
* Be strict and objective — do not default to high scores.
* "Manga/Script Follow" is the most important criterion — a visually polished
  candidate that depicts the wrong action/pose/camera framing is a bad candidate.
* "Character Count Accuracy" is a hard gate for FOREGROUND, individually-
  distinguishable characters only — an extra invented foreground person, a

  small, anonymous background crowds/populations in a wide shot that the
  script's own Environment/Description already calls for — those are normal
  scene content, never a reason to lower this score.
